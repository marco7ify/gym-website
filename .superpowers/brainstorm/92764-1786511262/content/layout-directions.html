C. Floating canvas inspector

Controls appear closest to the selected equipment. This is quick for simple actions, but the panel can obscure nearby machines and becomes awkward for longer forms.

My recommendation: A gives the best balance of speed, visibility, and room for the detailed controls your planner already has.
